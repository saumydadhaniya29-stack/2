Warranty

What is FlowGem's warranty period?

All FlowGem products come with a 36-Month (3 Year) warranty from the date of purchase, covering manufacturing defects in material and workmanship.

What does the warranty cover?

The warranty covers:

  • Manufacturing defects in the body, cartridge, or fittings
  • Premature failure under normal use conditions
  • Leakage due to faulty components (not installation errors)
What is NOT covered under warranty?

The warranty does not cover:

  • Damage due to improper installation
  • Damage caused by misuse, accidents, or negligence
  • Normal wear and tear (rubber washers, O-rings)
  • Damage due to excessive water pressure or chemical exposure
  • Cosmetic damage like scratches after installation

Installation

Can I install FlowGem taps myself?

FlowGem taps use universal fitting standards, making them compatible with standard Indian plumbing. Basic models like bib cocks and pillar taps can be installed by an experienced plumber easily. We recommend professional installation for wall mixers and shower sets.

What is the recommended water pressure for FlowGem products?

FlowGem products are tested to work optimally at 0.5 – 5 bar water pressure. We recommend using a pressure regulator if your supply exceeds 5 bar to avoid premature wear.

Material & Safety

What is PTMT and why is it better than brass?

PTMT (Poly Tetra Methylene Terephthalate) is an engineering-grade polymer. Compared to brass, PTMT is:

  • 100% lead-free — safe for drinking water
  • Rust-free — no corrosion over time
  • Lighter — easier to install
  • More affordable — no raw material price volatility
  • Eco-friendly — fully recyclable
Are FlowGem products safe for drinking water lines?

Absolutely. All FlowGem PTMT products are 100% lead-free and free from heavy metals. They are third-party lab tested and safe for direct use on drinking water supply lines.

Are your products BIS / ISI certified?

Yes. FlowGem products carry the BIS / ISI Mark and the company holds an ISO certification for its quality management system across manufacturing and supply chain operations.
