Our Story

From a Small Idea to India's Trusted Tap Brand

FlowGem Industries was founded in 2009 in Gujarat with one mission — to provide every Indian household with affordable, durable, and safe water fittings. What started as a small workshop with a handful of products has grown into a full-scale manufacturing facility with over 500 product variants.

We noticed that most brass fittings in the market were heavy, prone to rust, contained harmful lead, and were expensive for middle-class homeowners. PTMT changed everything. We adopted this engineering-grade polymer early, refined our manufacturing process, and never looked back.

"Every tap we make carries our promise — pure water, no compromise. That's the FlowGem way."

Today, FlowGem is trusted by builders, architects, plumbers, and homeowners across 20+ states in India. We operate from our state-of-the-art factory in Kuha, Gujarat, and supply to dealers and distributors pan-India.

Why FlowGem

What Makes Us Different

It's not just the product — it's the entire experience from factory to your home.

Own Manufacturing Facility

We own and operate our factory in Kuha, Gujarat — giving us full control over quality, pricing, and production timelines with no middlemen inflating costs.

In-House Quality Testing

Every batch undergoes pressure testing, drip checks, and material analysis before leaving our facility. We don't ship what we wouldn't use in our own homes.

Lead-Free Commitment

Since day one, every FlowGem product has been 100% lead-free. We believe safe drinking water is a right, not a feature — and our materials reflect that belief.

Fast Pan-India Delivery

With our Gujarat factory location and established logistics network, we deliver to dealers and distributors across 20+ states quickly and reliably.

36-Month Warranty

We stand behind every product with a 36-month warranty. Spare parts are available nationwide and our support team resolves issues without hassle.

Dealer-Friendly Pricing

Factory-direct margins make FlowGem the go-to brand for dealers looking for quality products they can confidently recommend to their customers at competitive prices.
